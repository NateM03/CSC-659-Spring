# How to Convert HW3 to Word and PDF

## Easiest Method

1. Open Microsoft Word.
2. Open `HW3_Report_Template.md`.
3. Word should convert the Markdown automatically.
4. Replace the placeholder name, e-mail, and date.
5. Write your own text for Section `2.1` and Section `3`.
6. Review spacing, bullets, and page lengths.
7. Save as PDF.
